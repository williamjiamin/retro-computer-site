Retro Computer

A retro desktop for iPhone, iPad and Mac. It opens in a 3D museum of five period rooms — a sixties machine room, an eighties study, a nineties office, a turn-of-the-century loft and a studio of today. Step up to any computer and you are inside it.

  • Thirteen eras, from a 1964 paper terminal to 2024 glass — each a real working desktop. Four are free.
  • 40 original games, three or more for every era, each drawn and scored in its own decade, with chip sound.
  • Private AI on your device: a built-in model that needs no network, and more open models to download.
  • The K-808 keyboard, Paint, a Unix-style Terminal, Notes, Calendar, Solitaire and more.
  • No ads, no tracking, no account.

Common questions

  • The assistant. A model is built into the app and answers with no network at all. More open models download from Chooser ▸ Local Model. On a Mac the Chooser also offers Apple Intelligence, a local Ollama server, or a cloud provider with your own API key (Pro).
  • Games. Every desktop has a Games icon with that era's shelf; All eras shows all 40. Tap a cover to play. On iPhone and iPad use the on-screen pad; on a Mac, the arrow keys with X and Z.
  • What Pro includes. The nine Pro eras, the Loft's and the Studio's computers, unlimited replies from the on-device models (15 a day are free), and on the Mac the cloud AI providers. One purchase, no subscription.
  • Restore a purchase. Open Eras, tap any era marked PRO, then Restore — or About This Computer ▸ Restore.
  • No sound. Check Control Panel ▸ Sounds and, on iPhone, the silent switch.
